常見問題

如何操作？

  • 手機：畫面底部左右兩個大按鈕分別是左、右擋板。發射前，右邊按鈕顯示「按住蓄力」，按住後在金色區放開即可發射；中間的「推」可輕推球台。
  • 電腦：使用鍵盤控制擋板與蓄力發射。

如何重玩隨機模式的球台？

隨機模式每局有一組 6 位英數編號。在標題畫面選擇輸入編號，即可重玩同一張球台。最近玩過的編號也會列在標題畫面。

遊戲需要網路嗎？

不需要。iOS 版是單機遊戲，沒有廣告、帳號或應用程式內購買。

我的成績會被上傳嗎？

不會。成績只儲存在你的裝置上。詳見隱私政策。

FAQ

How do I play?

  • On iPhone, the two large bottom buttons are the left and right flippers. Before launch, the right button reads "按住蓄力" (Hold to Charge): hold it and release inside the gold zone to launch. The small center button ("推") nudges the table.
  • On desktop, use the keyboard for the flippers and the charge-to-launch plunger.

How do I replay a Random Mode table?

Every Random Mode table has a 6-character code. Enter it from the title screen to replay the same table. Recently played codes are listed there too.

Does the game need internet?

No. The iOS version is an offline single-player game with no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.

Is my score uploaded?

No. Scores are stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
